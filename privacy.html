Petal & Page

Privacy notice

Last updated October 6, 2026

Petal & Page is a bullet journal. Your journal is personal, so we keep this simple: we store what we need to save your journal and let you sign in, and nothing else.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google.
  • Your journal: everything you write and set in the app (entries, trackers, themes, layout), saved to your account so it opens on any device.
  • As a guest: nothing leaves your device. Your pages are saved in your browser until you create an account.

Who can see your journal

Only you. Your journal is stored with database rules that let an account read and change only its own journal. We don't sell your information, show ads, or use your journal to train anything.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and journals and sends sign-in emails.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" if you choose it, and serves the site's fonts.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, it may keep basic server logs such as IP addresses.

Your choices

  • Export or back up your whole journal any time from Export in the app.
  • Sign out any time; signing out clears the journal from that device.
  • To delete your account and journal, email info@lifestages.us and we'll remove them.

Contact

Questions about privacy: info@lifestages.us.
